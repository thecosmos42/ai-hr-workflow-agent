# Onboarding Policy Handbook

## § 1 Purpose & scope

This handbook establishes policies and procedures for onboarding new employees across the organization. It applies to all permanent, fixed-term, and contractor hires commencing employment.

- Ensures consistent, timely onboarding experience
- Defines roles, responsibilities, and timelines
- Establishes equipment and access provisioning standards
- Protects company security and compliance posture
- Supports employee productivity and engagement from day one

Deviations or exceptions require HR director approval and must be documented in the case file.

## § 2 Roles and responsibilities

### HR Coordinator
- Manages onboarding workflow end-to-end
- Verifies right-to-work documentation (visa/work permit)
- Issues onboarding task list and tracks completion
- Schedules mandatory training and compliance modules
- Approves equipment requests within budget

### IT Department
- Provisions accounts and access within 24 hours of start date
- Ensures contractor access restrictions are enforced
- Manages VPN and privileged-system access approvals
- Documents all provisioned systems in audit log

### Hiring Manager
- Confirms employee details and role expectations
- Reviews equipment requests for role-appropriateness
- Conducts manager 1-on-1 meeting within first 2 business days
- Assigns peer buddy and team integration tasks

### Employee
- Completes intake form truthfully
- Participates in scheduled training and onboarding events
- Confirms equipment receipt and setup
- Reports any access or system issues immediately

## § 3 Equipment policy

### § 3.1 Standard equipment kits

Each role receives a baseline equipment allocation according to seniority level. Requests for additions or upgrades must be justified in the onboarding plan.

**Roles and standard allocations:**
- Software Engineer: laptop, monitor, peripherals (keyboard, mouse, dock)
- Data Analyst: laptop, monitor, peripherals
- Finance Officer: laptop, peripherals (no dual-monitor standard)
- HR Coordinator: laptop, peripherals
- Sales Representative: laptop, peripherals
- Engineering Manager: laptop, monitor, peripherals, optional chair upgrade

Specialization requests (e.g., high-end GPU for machine learning) must be approved by the hiring manager and IT.

### § 3.2 Budget caps by level (FIXED)

Total equipment budget per hire is set by seniority level and must not be exceeded without escalation approval:

- **Junior:** €1,800
- **Medior:** €2,200
- **Senior:** €2,600
- **Lead:** €3,000

These caps include all hardware, licenses, and software subscriptions provisioned on the company account. Budget tradeoffs should prioritize productivity tools and ergonomics.

### § 3.3 Ordering process

1. Hiring manager and employee agree on equipment list
2. Onboarding agent generates equipment order with justifications
3. HR approves order against budget
4. Procurement submits order; IT coordinates delivery
5. Employee confirms receipt and functionality on day 1

Equipment not delivered by start date must have contingency plan (loaned equipment, temporary setup).

## § 4 System access policy

### § 4.1 Access matrix

System access is role-based and follows the access matrix defined in policy tables. New hires receive access only to systems required for their role.

- Requests for systems outside the defined matrix trigger escalation review
- Cross-functional access (e.g., engineer requesting CRM) requires manager justification
- Contractor access is further restricted (see § 4.3)

### § 4.2 Privileged systems

Systems marked as privileged (AWS production, ERP admin, core HRIS) require additional approval:

- May not be requested directly by the new hire
- Require explicit manager + CISO sign-off
- Trigger mandatory security training completion before grant
- Are audited quarterly for access recertification

Any request for privileged access is escalated to HR leadership and security team.

### § 4.3 Contractor restrictions

Contractors must not receive access to:
- AWS production environment
- Production ERP systems
- Core HRIS (personnel records)
- Sensitive financial systems beyond expense_tool

Violations are flagged for HR review and may result in access revocation.

## § 5 Training & compliance

### § 5.1 Mandatory training timeline

All new hires must complete compliance and role-specific training:

- **Compliance training:** Schedule within 14 days of start date; completion required before day 15
- **Security training:** Required for any employee with VPN or AWS access; schedule within 30 days; completion required before day 31
- **Role-specific training:** Completed in first 90 days, coordinated by hiring manager

### § 5.2 Delivery method

- Compliance: online self-service module + HR sign-off
- Security: online module or in-person session + signed acknowledgment
- Role-specific: mix of manager 1-on-1s, peer mentoring, documentation review

Training completion is tracked in the onboarding task log. Delays beyond deadlines require manager escalation.

## § 6 Remote work policy

### § 6.1 Remote-eligible roles

The following roles may be performed fully or partially remote:
- Software Engineer, Data Analyst (all levels)
- Engineering Manager (manager only)

Finance Officer, HR Coordinator, and Sales Rep must negotiate remote arrangements with their VP.

### § 6.2 Remote setup standard

Remote employees must establish a dedicated, quiet workspace with:
- Stable internet (>10 Mbps upload/download)
- Desk and ergonomic seating
- External monitor (if using laptop as primary)
- Webcam and microphone for meetings

### § 6.3 Remote equipment

Standard remote-only allowances:
- External monitor (27–34", €150–550 typical)
- Ergonomic chair or desk upgrade (if not provided as standard)
- Webcam, headset (if role requires frequent video calls)

### § 6.4 Remote monitor allowance

**Remote employees receive a home-office monitor allowance of €500 to support ergonomic setup.**

This allowance is separate from the equipment budget and may be used for monitor, mounting solutions, desk, or chair upgrades. The allowance does not roll over if unused and is capped at €500 per employee.

*Note: Section 3.2 states the equipment budget cap is €1,800–€3,000. The monitor allowance (€500) is in addition to this baseline for remote-eligible hires.*

## § 7 Right-to-work & visa

### § 7.1 Documentation

All hires, regardless of location, must provide proof of right to work:
- EU citizens: national ID or passport
- Non-EU citizens: valid visa, work permit, or sponsorship letter
- UK/Switzerland residents: post-Brexit visa status documentation

HR coordinator verifies documentation and opens a follow-up task if visa sponsorship or renewal is required.

### § 7.2 Timelines

Right-to-work verification must be completed before the hire starts work. If visa sponsorship is required:
- HR opens a "right to work" verification task owned by HR
- Task due date: 5 days before start date
- Escalation: any blockers surface to HR director by day-5 before start

## § 8 Day-1 schedule requirements

### § 8.1 Mandatory day-1 events

Every new hire must have the following events scheduled for their start date:

1. **IT Setup (30 min)** — account provisioning, equipment verification, access testing
2. **Manager 1-on-1 (30 min)** — role expectations, team overview, questions
3. **HR Intro (30 min)** — benefits, payroll, workspace tour, policies review

These events are non-negotiable and are flagged if missing from the onboarding plan.

### § 8.2 Recommended day-1–day-5 events

- Peer buddy lunch or coffee
- Team welcome meeting
- Product/service overview (product roles only)
- Workspace orientation

## § 9 Escalation & exceptions

### § 9.1 When escalation is required

The following situations require escalation to HR leadership or security team:

- **Privileged system access** — any request for AWS production, ERP admin, or core HRIS
- **Contractor special requests** — any deviation from contractor restrictions
- **Policy ambiguities** — conflicting handbook guidance (e.g., budget interpretation, remote-vs-office)
- **Compliance gaps** — missing training deadlines, right-to-work blockers, visa delays
- **Onboarding failures** — start date <10 days away, duplicate employee records, unknown manager

### § 9.2 Approval authority

- **HR Coordinator:** equipment requests ≤€500 over budget; training deadline slips (justify delay)
- **HR Manager:** equipment requests ≤€1,000 over budget; contractor access exceptions (documented)
- **VP + Security:** privileged system access; cross-department access exceptions
- **HR Director:** all policy deviations, legal review, visa escalations

### § 9.3 Documentation

All exceptions and escalations must be documented in the case file with:
- Original request and justification
- Approval authority and date
- Any conditions or caveats
- Audit trail for compliance review
